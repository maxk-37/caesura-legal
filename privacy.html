Privacy Policy

App: Caesura
Effective date: 19 July 2026
Last updated: 19 July 2026

The short version

Caesura is a voice journal for the moments between tasks. It's built to keep what's most personal — your voice and your transcripts — on your phone, where it belongs.

  • Your voice recordings and transcripts never leave your device. They are stored locally in the iOS app sandbox. We have no copy. We cannot read them. We cannot recover them if you uninstall.
  • We collect a small amount of anonymous usage data — things like "a capture was started" or "the timeline was opened" — to understand whether the app is helping people and where it's getting stuck. This is sent to our analytics database (Supabase). It is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.
  • In the EU, EEA, and UK, this analytics is OFF by default. You have to turn it on in Settings if you want to share it. Everywhere else, it is ON by default and you can turn it off in Settings at any time.
  • You can delete everything we have on you with one button in Settings.
  • We do not advertise. We do not sell or share your data. We do not use it to train AI models.

If you want the details, they're below.

caesura.support@proton.me

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you can use this address to exercise your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

2. Data that stays on your device

The following information is created and stored only on your iPhone. It does not leave your device. We have no copy of it on any server.

  • Audio recordings of your voice captures.
  • Transcripts of those recordings.
  • Tags, edits, and any notes you attach to entries.
  • The full timeline of your captures.

This data lives in the app's local storage (SwiftData) and the iOS Keychain. If you uninstall Caesura, this data is deleted by iOS along with the app. We cannot recover it for you.

iOS may include this data in your device's iCloud backup if you have iCloud Backup enabled. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours, and is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple.

3. Data we do collect (anonymous usage analytics)

To understand whether Caesura is working — and where it isn't — we collect a small set of anonymous usage events. This is what we collect and what we do not collect.

What we collect

FieldExampleWhy
Pseudonymous device identifier A random UUID generated on your device on first launch (e.g. a1b2c3d4-…) To group events from the same device so we can compute things like "median captures per day per user"
Event type captureStarted, captureSaved, timelineOpened, promptShown, promptTapped To understand what people use and what they ignore
Capture source in-app, widget, shortcut, interstitial-prompt To know which entry points are actually used
Event timestamp An ISO-8601 timestamp from your device To compute trends over time
App version 1.0 To separate behavior across versions and to detect regressions
iOS version iOS 26.0 To diagnose OS-specific issues
Pro entitlement status true / false To distinguish free-tier behavior from paid behavior in aggregate

What we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or Apple ID.
  • Your voice recordings or any audio data.
  • Your transcripts or the contents of any entry.
  • Your location (we do not request location permissions).
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, or any other personal data on your device.
  • Any advertising or tracking identifiers (we do not use the Apple Advertising Identifier).
  • Any data from third parties.

Legal basis (EU/EEA/UK)

For users in the EU, EEA, and UK, our legal basis for processing this analytics data is your consent under GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and the relevant national implementations of the ePrivacy Directive (EU) and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (UK). You provide that consent by toggling analytics ON in Settings. You can withdraw it at any time by toggling it back OFF.

For users outside those regions, the legal basis is our legitimate interest under GDPR Article 6(1)(f) in improving the app and understanding how it is used, balanced against the minimal nature of the data and the fact that no personal identifiers are collected. You can object to this processing at any time by toggling analytics OFF in Settings.

4. Default behavior — region-aware

When you first launch Caesura, we set the analytics toggle as follows, based on the region your iPhone is configured for (Settings → General → Language & Region):

  • EU member states, EEA (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway), and the United Kingdom: analytics is OFF by default. You can turn it on in Settings → Privacy → Anonymous analytics.
  • Everywhere else: analytics is ON by default. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy → Anonymous analytics.

This split exists because EU and UK consent rules require prior, informed, opt-in consent for non-essential analytics. Outside those jurisdictions, the legal regime allows opt-out.

The default is computed once on first launch and saved. If you later travel or change your device's region, the toggle does not flip — your previous choice is respected. You can always change it manually in Settings.

5. How long we keep analytics data

Analytics events are kept for a maximum of 90 days from the date the event occurred. After that, they are deleted from the analytics database. This applies to all users, regardless of region.

We may keep aggregated counts (for example, "total daily active devices in March") indefinitely. Aggregated counts cannot be linked back to any individual device.

6. Where the data is stored

Analytics data is stored in Supabase, a hosted Postgres provider. Our project is configured in the United States — East US (Ohio, AWS us-east-2) region.

If you are in the EU/EEA/UK and our project is hosted in the US, the transfer to the United States relies on Supabase's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) under GDPR Article 46. If you would prefer your data not to be transferred, you can keep analytics OFF (which is the default in your region anyway) or use the "Delete my analytics data" button to remove anything that has already been sent.

We do not use any other third-party processors for the analytics pipeline. The data is not shared with anyone.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

Available to all users — directly in the app

  • Turn analytics off at any time: Settings → Privacy → Anonymous analytics.
  • Delete everything we have on you: Settings → Privacy → Delete my analytics data. This sends a delete request to our database, removes any local copy of your event log, and rotates your pseudonymous device identifier so that any future events (if you re-enable analytics) cannot be linked to anything from before.

Additional rights for EU/EEA/UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Rectify inaccurate data (Article 16).
  • Erase your data (Article 17) — the in-app delete button satisfies this, but you can also email us.

  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21).

To exercise any of these by email rather than in-app, write to us at the address in Section 1. To verify the request, you'll need to include the pseudonymous identifier shown in the in-app debug panel (Settings → tap the version number five times → "Copy user ID").

You have the right to know what we collect (this section), the right to delete (the in-app button), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising those rights. We will not deny service, charge different prices, or degrade quality based on a privacy choice.

8. Children

Caesura is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete any associated data.

9. Security

Analytics data in transit is encrypted with TLS. At rest, it is stored in Postgres on infrastructure managed by Supabase (which uses AES-256 encryption at rest by default). Access to the database is restricted to the developer using a service role key that is not shipped in the app.

We have implemented row-level security so that the public app credentials can only insert events — they cannot read, modify, or delete any data. This means a copy of the app cannot be used to scrape data about other users.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours where required by law.

10. Kill switch

We maintain a remote kill switch that can disable analytics collection across all installs of the app within one launch cycle. We use this if a privacy issue is discovered or if the analytics infrastructure needs to be paused. When the kill switch is active, no events are sent regardless of your in-app setting.

11. In-app purchases

Caesura sells a one-time "Pro" unlock through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never see your payment method, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple shares anonymized purchase information with us (via App Store Server Notifications) so that the app can verify your purchase. To allow us to correlate Pro entitlement status with the analytics event log without linking to your Apple ID, we attach the same pseudonymous device identifier described in Section 3 to the StoreKit transaction (appAccountToken). Apple's privacy policy, not ours, governs the payment data: apple.com/legal/privacy.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if we change what we collect, why we collect it, where it is stored, or how long we keep it. The "Last updated" date at the top will change whenever we make a substantive update. For material changes — for example, adding a new category of data — we will surface a notice in the app on next launch and require you to re-confirm your analytics choice.

We will not retroactively apply a less protective policy to data that was collected under a more protective one.
